The Lab
Everything your character can do on one screen, a workbench for building something new, and every quirk in the campaign to check you have not reinvented one. The database names the quirks, never the people who have them.

Nothing on this page changes a character. Drafts are kept in this browser only, and Show as JSON hands over the ability in the exact shape a character file takes, so your DM can paste it in if they agree with it. The averages are measured by rolling the expression a few thousand times through the toolkit's own dice engine, not worked out from a formula — which is why they wobble by a tenth and why they are right for 4d6kh3.

Nobody is named in the quirk database. It is assembled from every character the campaign knows — which includes the gang, the Underground and the villains — so entries are keyed by the quirk rather than by who has it, two people who share one are a single entry, and the descriptions say the user where they used to say a name. Searching for a person finds nothing. If you want the cast, that is the Characters tab, behind the DM block.
